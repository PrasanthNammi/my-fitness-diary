# Cycle & Health: privacy note

Plain-language notice for the Cycle & Health section of My Fitness Diary (MFD). The same text is shown in the app
(Settings → Cycle & Health → How your data is handled). Draft for review; not legal advice.

## What it is

An optional, private section for women (profile sex: female) to record periods, symptoms, mood, medicines, lab results
(e.g. thyroid tests) and conditions (e.g. PCOS, hypothyroidism), see patterns and an estimate of the next period.
It is **off** until you switch it on. It is not a medical device: it does not diagnose, interpret lab results, show
fertile days or ovulation, or help prevent or plan pregnancy.

## What is stored

Only what you enter (or allow from Health Connect): daily logs (bleeding, flow, pain, symptoms, mood, energy, notes,
and fertility observations such as LH tests, which are recorded only), medicines and doses, lab results with each lab's
range, conditions, and your Cycle & Health settings.

## Why, and on what basis

To show your records back to you, calculate your own patterns and estimate on your phone, remind you if you ask,
and make a report or export when you ask. The legal basis is your **explicit consent** (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a); India DPDP
Act consent), given by switching Cycle & Health on. You can withdraw it at any time by switching it off or deleting
the data; withdrawal does not affect what happened before.

## Who can see it

- **Only you.** It is never shown to friends or in the Friends feed.
- **AI assistants** (ChatGPT, Claude) only if you do **both**: tick "Cycle & Health" when connecting them, and switch
  on "Let AI assistants read this" in MFD. Then the assistant's provider receives what it reads under its own privacy
  terms. Switching it off in MFD stops every connected assistant at once.
- **Health Connect**: if you switch on "Periods from Health Connect", MFD reads period days on your phone. MFD never
  writes cycle data to Health Connect.
- Nobody else. MFD does not sell data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

## Where it is kept

In MFD's database (Supabase, EU region: Frankfurt), readable only by your account (row-level security), and in MFD's
nightly encrypted backups (kept 30 days). Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the MFD Android app; nothing about
them goes through a server, and the lock screen shows only "MFD · Reminder".

## How long

Until you delete it. "Delete all cycle data" removes every record at once; deleting your MFD account removes everything.
Deleted data leaves the encrypted backups within 30 days.

## Your rights

Access and copy (Settings → Cycle & Health → Export my cycle data, or the report for your doctor), correction (edit any
entry), deletion (delete one entry, or everything), withdrawal of consent (switch off), and complaint to a data
protection authority. Ask the MFD owner through the app for anything else.

## If something goes wrong

If health data were ever exposed, affected people would be told without undue delay, as required (GDPR Art. 33–34;
US FTC Health Breach Notification Rule; India DPDP Act).
